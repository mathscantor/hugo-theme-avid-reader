A short, clean nightmare. Wells sends a Victorian inventor far enough forward that class has become species. The Eloi are pretty and helpless; the Morlocks keep the lights on. The future is a garden with a machine room underneath.

The book’s chill is not the monsters. It is the beach at the end of the world, where even those distinctions have worn away. I like how little comfort the Time Traveller brings home. He has a story and a few wilted flowers.

{{< pullquote attribution="The Time Traveller" >}}
It is a law of nature we overlook, that intellectual versatility is the compensation for change, danger, and trouble.
{{< /pullquote >}}

Science fiction as a brief, unsparing fable. It still fits in an evening and stays in the week.
