Shelley wrote a laboratory story that is really about abandonment. Victor wants the glory of making life and none of the work of living with what he made. The creature wants language, company, and a name that is not a curse.

The nested narrators keep the horror at a slight distance, which makes the loneliness louder. Alpine ice, Arctic ice, the same white refusal. I expected lightning. I found letters, lectures, and a child learning the world from stolen books.

{{< pullquote attribution="The creature" >}}
I was benevolent and good; misery made me a fiend.
{{< /pullquote >}}

Still the most convincing argument that a scientist’s first duty is not discovery but care.
