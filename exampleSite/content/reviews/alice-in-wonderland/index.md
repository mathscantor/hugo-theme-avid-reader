Carroll’s joke is that childhood logic is stricter than adult logic. Alice keeps trying to apply rules — size, manners, croquet — and Wonderland keeps changing the definitions. The book is short, which is mercy. A longer Wonderland would become a lecture.

What holds it together is Alice’s stubbornness. She is curious, often rude, and rarely impressed. The tea party is still the funniest scene in the language for people who have sat through a meeting.
