Stevenson understood that a treasure map is a plot engine. Jim Hawkins wants to be useful and is repeatedly used. The Hispaniola is a school for adult motives: greed, loyalty, the pleasure of being in a story.

Long John Silver is the reason the book lasts. He is charming, murderous, and never fully caught by the moral the other characters want him to occupy. The apple barrel scene is still a perfect piece of eavesdropping.

{{< pullquote attribution="Long John Silver" >}}
Them that die'll be the lucky ones.
{{< /pullquote >}}

Brisk, salty, and honest about how adventure feels when you are not quite in charge of it.
